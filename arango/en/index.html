Learn in 3 minutes

Each product, explained simply.

The most affordable option

Term life insurance

Covers your family for a set period (10, 20 or 30 years). It is the most affordable option and works to cover the mortgage, debts and the years when your children depend on you most. If nothing happens, the protection ends when the term is up.

Indexed Universal Life

IUL: protection + savings

Indexed Universal Life is permanent insurance whose cash value is tied to the performance of a market index, with a floor that protects against losses and a cap that limits gains. You can use that money during your lifetime for your children’s college, a business or your retirement.

To leave everything taken care of

Final expense

A smaller life insurance policy meant to cover the funeral and last expenses, so your family doesn’t pay them out of pocket or raise money. The payment and the coverage depend on your age and the insurance company.

For your retirement

Annuities

You give an insurance company a sum of money and, in exchange, it pays you an income for a set time or for life. It helps complement Social Security and gives you a fixed payment in retirement. We explain the types and their terms before you decide.

  • It is legal and regulated

    Insurance companies and agents hold state licenses. You can verify any license with your state’s department of insurance.

  • Tax-free benefit

    In general, the death benefit your family receives is not considered taxable income. Every case is reviewed with your accountant.

  • You decide the amount

    Together we set a monthly payment you can keep up without squeezing your budget.
